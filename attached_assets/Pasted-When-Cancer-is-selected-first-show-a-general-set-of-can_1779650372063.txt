When "Cancer" is selected, first show a general set of cancer questions, then reveal 
a SECOND dropdown labeled "Which type?" with: Breast, Lung, Colorectal, Prostate, 
Skin / Melanoma, Cervical, Leukemia, Lymphoma. Selecting a type adds a few 
type-specific questions below the general ones.

GENERAL CANCER QUESTIONS
- Diagnosis: What type and stage is it? What tests confirmed it? Where is it, and has 
  it spread?
- Treatment options: What are ALL my options (surgery, chemo, radiation, immunotherapy, 
  targeted therapy, watchful waiting)? What do you recommend and why? Is the goal to 
  cure it or control it?
- Side effects: What are the side effects of each option? Will it affect fertility? 
  Any long-term effects?
- Logistics: How long is treatment? How will it affect work and daily life? What about 
  cost and insurance? How do I get a second opinion?
- Support: Are there clinical trials? Can I see palliative care for symptom support? 
  Are there support groups? Who is my main point of contact?
- Follow-up: How will we know it's working? What's the scan schedule? What are signs 
  of recurrence?

TYPE-SPECIFIC (add a few each)
- Breast: What's my hormone-receptor and HER2 status? Lumpectomy or mastectomy? Should 
  I get genetic (BRCA) testing?
- Lung: Was it caused by smoking, and does that change treatment? What's my mutation 
  testing showing? Am I a surgery candidate?
- Colorectal: Do I need a colostomy? Was the tumor tested for genetic markers? What 
  about my family's screening?
- Prostate: What's my Gleason score and PSA? Is active surveillance an option for me? 
  How will treatment affect urinary and sexual function?
- Skin / Melanoma: How deep is it? Do we need to check my lymph nodes? How often should 
  I have full-body skin checks?
- Cervical: What stage and HPV status? How will treatment affect fertility? What 
  follow-up Pap/HPV schedule?
- Leukemia: Which type (acute/chronic)? Do I need treatment now or monitoring? Will I 
  need a bone marrow transplant?
- Lymphoma: Hodgkin or non-Hodgkin, and which subtype? What's the treatment plan and 
  timeline? What are the long-term effects?

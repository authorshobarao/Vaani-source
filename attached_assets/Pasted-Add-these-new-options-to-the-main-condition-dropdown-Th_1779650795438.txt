Add these new options to the main condition dropdown: "Thyroid condition", 
"PCOS / gynecological", and "Mental health (depression / anxiety)". Use the same 
category-grouped, checkable, printable, saved structure as the other conditions.

THYROID CONDITION
- Understanding it: Is my thyroid overactive or underactive? What were my TSH, T3, and 
  T4 levels? What caused this (for example, Hashimoto's or Graves')? Is it permanent?
- Treatment: What medication do I need, and at what dose? How long until I feel better? 
  What if I still feel off after starting it?
- Monitoring: How often do we recheck my levels? What symptoms mean my dose is wrong?
- Daily life: How should I take my medication (timing, food, other meds)? Does this 
  affect my weight, energy, mood, hair, or periods? Does it affect pregnancy or fertility?
- Follow-up: When is my next blood test and visit?

PCOS / GYNECOLOGICAL
- Understanding it: What's causing my symptoms? What tests confirm it (hormones, 
  ultrasound)? 
- Symptoms & treatment: What are my options for irregular periods, acne, or unwanted 
  hair? Do I need birth control, metformin, or something else? What are the side effects?
- Fertility: How does this affect my ability to get pregnant? What can help if I want 
  to conceive — now or later?
- Long-term health: What's my risk for diabetes, heart issues, or other problems, and 
  how do we watch for them?
- Lifestyle: Do diet, weight, or exercise changes help, and how much?
- For general gynecological visits: Do I need a Pap smear or HPV test, and when? Is 
  this bleeding/pain/discharge normal? When should I come back?
- Follow-up & second opinions: When do we recheck? How do I get a referral if I want one?

MENTAL HEALTH (DEPRESSION / ANXIETY)
- Understanding it: What do you think is going on? Could anything physical (thyroid, 
  vitamins, sleep, medications) be contributing?
- Options: What are my treatment options — therapy, medication, or both? What do you 
  recommend and why? How long until I might feel a difference?
- Medication: What are the side effects? Is it habit-forming? What if the first one 
  doesn't work? How and when would I stop it safely?
- Therapy & support: What kind of therapy fits me, and how do I find someone? Are there 
  support groups or low-cost options?
- Daily life & follow-up: What can I do day to day? When should we check in again? Who 
  do I contact if things get worse before then?

SAFETY: At the very top of the Mental Health section, show a calm, supportive box:
"If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, you deserve help right now. 
In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). If you're elsewhere, contact 
your local emergency number or a crisis line." (Make this easy to localize later.)

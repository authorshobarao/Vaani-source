The user identified **Your Voice First** as Vaani's operator. Do not infer a country of incorporation, privacy email, or registered legal suffix from that name.

**Why:** The operator identity was explicitly supplied; jurisdiction and a dedicated privacy email were not. Older founder attribution is not authority to substitute a different legal operator.

**How to apply:** Use the confirmed operator identity. Keep privacy statements tied to implemented data handling; do not promise consent controls, retention deadlines, provider zero-retention/training restrictions, or self-service deletion without verifying them. Policy changes must also cover pre-rendered non-JavaScript pages and metadata, not just the hydrated page.
